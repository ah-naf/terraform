Why a NAT gateway per zone

With one NAT in AZ1, a zone 1 outage cuts outbound internet for zone 2 as well. Zone 2 traffic also crosses zones to reach the NAT, which adds data transfer cost. A NAT in each zone keeps each zone independent. Each NAT needs its own Elastic IP and sits in the public subnet of the same zone.
